Savings consultants

A savings consultant helps a business identify opportunities to reduce spending and compare suppliers. These consultants work with businesses to identify areas where cost savings can be achieved, then research and negotiate with vendors.

  1. Vendor research

    Savings consultants research vendors and suppliers for products and services a business needs. This may involve evaluating multiple vendors, comparing prices and service offerings, and identifying hidden costs.

  2. Negotiation

    Once potential vendors have been identified, consultants negotiate on behalf of the business. This may include pricing, payment terms, or other concessions that can help the business save money.

  3. Contract review

    Consultants review vendor contracts for fees or clauses that could be detrimental to the business. They may negotiate changes to better align terms with the business's needs.

  4. Ongoing savings analysis

    Consultants monitor vendor contracts and pricing and periodically review expenses to identify new opportunities for savings.

A savings consultant can help a business compare services and costs, negotiate contract terms, and monitor expenses. Results depend on the business's existing arrangements and the provider's engagement.

Debt brokers

A debt broker is a professional who acts as an intermediary between a business seeking financing and lenders or investors willing to provide debt financing. Debt brokers help businesses identify potential lenders and negotiate financing terms.

  1. Financial analysis

    Debt brokers review financial statements to understand the business's position and funding needs. They may also assess how its credit profile affects borrowing options.

  2. Lender or investor research

    A broker can look for funding sources suited to the business, including banks, alternative lenders, or investors.

  3. Negotiation

    Brokers may negotiate interest rates, repayment terms, collateral requirements, and other financing terms on behalf of the business.

  4. Closing the deal

    Brokers help a business navigate the financing process, coordinate documentation, and work toward completing the transaction.

A debt broker can help a business identify potential sources of financing and navigate the process. Financing availability and terms depend on the lender, the business, and the transaction.

Investor matchmaking

An investor matchmaking service connects investors with businesses seeking funding. These services act as intermediaries, helping businesses find investors suited to their financing needs.

  1. Investor research

    Services research potential investors who may be interested in the business, including venture capitalists, angel investors, private equity firms, or other types of investors.

  2. Business analysis

    The service reviews financials, the management team, products or services, and market positioning to assess suitability for different investors.

  3. Pitch development

    The service helps develop a pitch that highlights the business's strengths, investment potential, and financing needs. This may include a business plan, financial projections, and supporting information.

  4. Introduction and facilitation

    The service introduces the business to potential investors and may help schedule meetings, coordinate due diligence, and discuss financing terms.
