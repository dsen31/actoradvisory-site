Lease or buy the right equipment.

A business may need to lease or purchase a large printer/scanner when it has a high volume of printing and scanning needs. Consider the equipment, the agreement, and the support that comes with it.

Factors to consider

  1. Printing and scanning volume

    With a consistently high volume, purchasing may be cost-effective. If needs fluctuate, leasing may be a better option.

  2. Budget

    Purchasing a large printer/scanner can involve a significant upfront cost. Leasing may offer more flexibility in how expenses are budgeted.

  3. Maintenance

    When a business purchases equipment, it is responsible for maintenance and repairs. A lease may include these services as part of the agreement.

  4. Technology upgrades

    Technology is constantly evolving. Leasing may provide more flexibility to upgrade equipment as needs change.

  5. Space

    Include the equipment's space requirements in the lease-or-purchase decision.

  6. Scalability

    If printing and scanning needs increase, a lease may allow a business to upgrade to larger equipment without purchasing another machine.

Businesses should consider volume, budget, maintenance and repair costs, upgrades, space, and scalability. Leasing may provide flexibility, while purchasing may provide more control over equipment and potentially lower costs over time.

Choosing a printer provider

  1. Available options

    Depending on location and industry, there may be a limited number of vendors offering the equipment your business needs.

  2. Quality and reliability

    It can be difficult to evaluate equipment before leasing or buying it. Customer reviews or a trial period may help assess quality and reliability.

  3. Cost

    Prices vary with the vendor, equipment, and agreement. Compare options to understand the full cost.

  4. Compatibility

    Check that the printer/scanner works with your existing equipment and software systems, particularly when considering a new vendor or nonstandard equipment.

  5. Service and support

    Reliable service matters when equipment develops problems. Research the vendor's support offering and whether it meets your needs.

  6. Contract terms

    Leasing or purchasing often involves a contract. Review the terms carefully before committing.

Finding the right provider involves comparing equipment quality, price, compatibility, support, and contract terms against your business's specific requirements.
