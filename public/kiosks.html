Help customers help themselves.

A kiosk is a self-service interactive system that can be used for a variety of purposes in a business. Kiosks with a tablet or TV can support the following uses.

Where a kiosk can help

  1. Customer check-in

    A kiosk can support check-in at hotels, airports, and medical offices. Customers can check themselves in, view reservation or appointment information, and print a boarding pass or appointment confirmation.

  2. Product catalog

    A kiosk can showcase products or services in a retail or showroom setting. Customers can browse products, view images or videos, and place orders directly from the kiosk.

  3. Wayfinding

    A kiosk can provide directions on corporate campuses, in hospitals, or in shopping malls. Visitors can enter a destination and receive directions to help them navigate the space.

  4. Informational displays

    Museums, trade shows, and corporate events can use kiosks to display text, images, videos, or interactive content that engages visitors and provides information about the business or event.

With suitable design and content, a kiosk can improve the visitor experience and make everyday interactions more efficient. Consider which tasks people should be able to complete before choosing a provider.
