Explore clean energy for your business.

Solar energy for businesses involves using photovoltaic (PV) systems to generate electricity from sunlight. These systems typically consist of solar panels mounted on a building's roof or a ground-mounted support structure, and an inverter that converts the panels' DC power into usable AC power for the building's electrical system.

Businesses can use solar energy to power their operations and reduce reliance on traditional grid-supplied electricity. This can help lower energy costs and reduce their carbon footprint. Solar energy can be particularly beneficial for businesses with high energy demands or those operating in areas with high electricity rates.

Solar energy can support sustainability goals and a business's reputation as well as its energy needs.

EV charging stations

Electric vehicle charging stations enable customers, employees, or other visitors to recharge their vehicles on site. They typically consist of a charging unit and a connection cable that plugs into the vehicle's charging port.

  1. Attracting customers

    Offering EV charging stations can attract customers who own electric vehicles and may be more likely to patronize businesses that offer charging options.

  2. Supporting sustainability

    Providing EV charging stations can help businesses demonstrate their commitment to sustainability and support the transition to cleaner transportation.

  3. Enhancing reputation

    Offering EV charging stations can enhance a business's reputation as a forward-thinking, customer-friendly organization.

  4. Generating revenue

    Some businesses may be able to charge customers for using their charging stations, which can provide a new source of revenue.

  5. Employee benefits

    Businesses with EV charging stations can offer a convenient amenity for employees who own electric vehicles, which can be valuable for attracting and retaining talent.

Consider the number of stations needed, connectors for the local market, charging speed, equipment and installation costs, and any required permits or regulatory approvals.

Choosing a clean energy provider

  1. Technical complexity

    Solar energy and EV charging projects require specialized knowledge. Business owners may lack the technical expertise to evaluate vendors and determine the best option.

  2. Upfront costs

    These projects can require a significant investment, making it challenging to compare providers and justify the cost.

  3. Vendor availability

    Depending on location, there may be limited choices of solar or charging-station providers.

  4. Warranties and maintenance

    Limited warranties or maintenance options can increase the risk of downtime and affect the return on investment.

  5. Permits and approvals

    Projects may require permits and regulatory approvals. Ask how a provider will handle local requirements, timelines, and associated costs.
