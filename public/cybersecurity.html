Protect your systems and information.

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting digital devices, networks, and sensitive information from unauthorized access, theft, damage, or other malicious attacks. Cybersecurity measures can include various tools, technologies, and best practices such as firewalls, encryption, two-factor authentication, password management, regular software updates, and employee training programs. The goal is to prevent cyber attacks and minimize the impact of security incidents that may occur.

Areas a cybersecurity provider may address

  1. Protection and detection

    Firewalls, antivirus software, malware protection, data encryption, intrusion detection and prevention systems, and network security protocols.

  2. Identity and access

    Two-factor authentication, user access controls, and mobile device management.

  3. Preparation and response

    Cybersecurity awareness training, incident response planning, and disaster recovery planning.

  4. Assessment and monitoring

    Vulnerability scanning and assessment, penetration testing, and security information and event management (SIEM).

  5. Systems and governance

    Cloud security, physical security measures, cybersecurity policies and procedures, risk management, and compliance and regulatory requirements.

This list is not exhaustive. The appropriate combination depends on the organization's systems, information, and business needs.

Frameworks, models, and requirements

The source material identifies the following approaches and requirements as topics to discuss with a qualified provider. The appropriate approach depends on your organization's size, industry, and specific needs.

  1. NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)

    A risk-based approach to managing and reducing cybersecurity risks.

  2. ISO/IEC 27001

    An information security management standard for managing and protecting sensitive information.

  3. CIS Controls and SANS Critical Security Controls

    Prioritized security practices for protecting IT systems and data. Discuss the current controls and the historical terminology with your provider.

  4. COBIT

    A framework for governing and managing IT processes and systems.

  5. ITIL

    Guidance for managing IT services and processes, including security-related service practices.

  6. FAIR

    An approach to measuring and managing information risk through quantitative analysis.

  7. OCTAVE

    A risk-based approach focused on critical business assets and processes.

  8. Government requirements and CMMC

    Government and defense supply-chain work can involve specific cybersecurity requirements. Ask a qualified provider which requirements apply to your contracts.

  9. Zero Trust

    A security approach centered on verifying access to users, devices, and resources.

  10. HIPAA

    Healthcare organizations may need support protecting electronic patient information and addressing applicable administrative, physical, and technical safeguards.

Choosing the right cybersecurity vendor

  1. Evolving threats

    Threats and attack methods change. Ask how a provider keeps its services and expertise relevant to the risks your business faces.

  2. Complexity

    Cybersecurity solutions can be complex and require specific technical expertise to deploy and maintain. Businesses must understand their security needs and capabilities to evaluate vendors effectively.

  3. Comparing approaches

    Vendors may address the same problem differently, which can make their offerings difficult to compare.

  4. Trustworthiness

    Evaluate the vendor's reputation, track record, and alignment with your security policies and compliance requirements.

  5. Cost and budget

    Cybersecurity solutions can be costly. The most expensive solution may not always be the best option; balance cost with the level of security required.

Businesses should research providers, evaluate their security needs, and choose a vendor that can provide appropriate protection at a reasonable cost. No provider can guarantee that an attack will never happen.
