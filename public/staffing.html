Find the right staffing model.

A staffing company, also known as a staffing agency or a recruitment agency, provides businesses with employees or temporary workers to fill open positions. Staffing companies typically specialize in a particular industry or job function and offer a range of staffing models to meet the needs of their clients.

Common staffing models

  1. Temporary staffing

    In this model, the staffing company provides workers for short-term assignments. This can be ideal for businesses that need extra help during peak periods or to cover staff shortages due to illness or vacation.

  2. Contract staffing

    This model provides businesses with workers for longer-term assignments or specific projects. The staffing company takes care of the administrative and HR functions, such as payroll, benefits, and taxes.

  3. Temp-to-perm or temp-to-hire

    The staffing agency provides temporary workers to the business. If the business is satisfied with the worker's performance, it may offer them a permanent position. The staffing agency may charge a fee if the business hires the worker permanently.

  4. Direct hire

    The staffing agency recruits candidates on behalf of the business for permanent positions. The agency may charge a fee for the recruitment process, which is usually a percentage of the new hire's salary.

  5. Retained search

    Executive recruitment can involve an upfront search fee that is payable regardless of whether the search results in a hire.

  6. Contingent search

    In this model, the staffing agency is paid a fee only if a candidate is hired. The fee is usually a percentage of the candidate's first-year salary.

  7. Recruitment process outsourcing (RPO)

    The staffing agency takes over some or all of the recruitment processes for the business. The agency is paid a fee for its services, which may be based on a fixed monthly fee or a per-hire fee.

Overall, staffing companies can provide businesses with a range of staffing models to meet their specific needs. By outsourcing staffing functions, businesses can save time and resources on recruitment and HR functions and focus on their core business operations.
